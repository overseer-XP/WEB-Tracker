# Bespoke brand page: design review, 23 Oct 2026

Reviewed the live Bespoke page as a design and process reviewer, using the live data pulled on 23 Oct.

## What the page felt like before this change

| Area | What a person sees | Why it hurts |
|---|---|---|
| Top of page | 5 stacked bands (status line, red blocked-by line, buttons, facts, next step box). About 430 px before any work shows | On a laptop, the sections start near the fold. The eye lands on summary text, not on what to do |
| Section switch | 6 thin grey text tabs, same weight as body text | Reads as a sentence, not as navigation. People miss Platform access, SOW and Best Practices |
| Tab labels | "Platform access (1 / 2)", "Onboarding steps (83%)" | Numbers without meaning. 1 / 2 of what? Good or bad? |
| Tasks | 7 team cards of equal weight, grey "On track" tags, small "+ 3 more" link | Answers who, not why or when. Cards do not look clickable |
| Time | No view of time at all | Bespoke is a live brand with launches. Nobody can see what lands when |
| Dependencies | Blocked items scattered: task blockers, access, SOW, the next step text | No single place answers "what is holding this launch up" |

## What changed (version 17)

| Change | What it fixes |
|---|---|
| Section tiles instead of tabs | Six large tiles, each with its own number and a coloured edge (red, amber, green, blue). Stays pinned while scrolling |
| Shorter header | SOW date moved to its tile. Next step is one line. The sections start about 60 px higher |
| Campaigns first | Tasks open on campaign cards: what it is for, dates, days left, progress, blocked and late, dependencies, teams |
| Campaign page | What it is for, at a glance, timeline by team, key dependencies, team cards for that campaign only |
| Team cards | Coloured team edge, big open count, hover lift, "See all 6 open ›" call to action |
| By team still there | One click back to the old team cards for people who think in teams |

## What the data says about Bespoke

| Finding | Number | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Tasks with no start or due date | 36 of 59 | They cannot show on a timeline. The campaign page lists them under the chart so they get dated |
| Tasks with dates in the past and still open | 6 late | Shoot planning (Plumpicity) is 39 days late. Either the date or the status is out of date |
| Campaign end dates passed with work still open | Batch 1 (30 Sept), Evergreen shoot (28 Sept) | Dates need a reset or the campaign needs closing |
| Brand Live but SOW not signed | SOW at Revisions, target 9 Oct, overdue | Commercial risk on a live brand. Now shows red on the SOW tile and as a dependency on each campaign |
| Sprint | TBC | The sprint field adds nothing yet. Fill it or drop it |
| Onboarding 83% on a Live brand | P4 Founder Workshops open | Either finish P4 or mark the leftover steps N/A, so the tile stops looking unfinished |
| Step owners | Most steps say No owner | Step lists cannot drive My tasks without owners |
| Status words | Pending, Briefed, In Progress, Complete, Blocked | Pending and Briefed mean different things to different people. Agree one meaning each |
